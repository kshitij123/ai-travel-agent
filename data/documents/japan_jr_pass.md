# Japan Rail Pass (JR Pass) Guide

The Japan Rail Pass is a discounted ticket for unlimited travel on most JR trains across Japan, including the Shinkansen bullet train (except Nozomi and Mizuho services).

## Prices (as of 2024, ordinary class)

| Duration | Price (JPY) | Approx. USD |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| 7 days   | 50,000      | ~$335       |
| 14 days  | 80,000      | ~$535       |
| 21 days  | 100,000     | ~$670       |

Green Car (first class) passes cost roughly 70% more than ordinary passes.

## Who can buy it

The JR Pass is only available to foreign tourists visiting Japan on a temporary visitor visa (tourist status). Japanese residents and long-term visa holders are not eligible.

## Where to buy

- **Outside Japan**: Buy an exchange order online or from an authorized agent, then exchange it for the actual pass at a JR ticket office in Japan within 3 months.
- **Inside Japan**: Limited trial sales at select prices (often slightly higher than overseas purchase).

## Is it worth it?

A 7-day ordinary pass costs ¥50,000. A one-way Tokyo → Kyoto Shinkansen ticket is roughly ¥13,000–14,000. If you take more than 2–3 long-distance JR trips in 7 days, the pass usually pays off.

## Coverage

Includes: most JR local and limited express trains, Shinkansen (Hikari, Sakura, Kodama), JR buses in some areas, and the JR ferry to Miyajima.

Excludes: Nozomi/Mizuho Shinkansen, private railway lines, subways, and highway buses.

## Activation

The pass is activated on first use or at a JR office. Choose your start date carefully — consecutive calendar days begin from activation.

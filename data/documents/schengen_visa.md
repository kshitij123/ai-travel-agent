# Schengen Visa Basics for Indian Travelers

The Schengen visa allows travel across 27 European countries with a single visa.

## Visa fee

| Applicant type        | Fee (EUR) |
|-----------------------|-----------|
| Adults (12+)          | 90        |
| Children (6–12)       | 45        |
| Children under 6      | Free      |

Fees are paid in local currency at the application center. Service charges from VFS/TLS add roughly ₹1,500–2,500 on top.

## Processing time

Standard processing: 15 calendar days. Apply at least 4–6 weeks before travel during peak season (May–September).

## Required documents (typical)

- Valid passport (issued within 10 years, valid 3 months beyond return)
- Completed application form and photos
- Travel insurance (minimum €30,000 coverage)
- Flight and hotel bookings
- Bank statements (last 3–6 months)
- Employment letter or business documents
- Cover letter explaining itinerary

## Tips

Apply through the embassy/consulate of your main destination country (where you spend the most nights). If visiting multiple countries equally, apply at the first entry country.
